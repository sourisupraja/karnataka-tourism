Why Karnataka

History, landscapes & culture

A compact guide to some of the state's most interesting travel experiences.

Karnataka combines ancient architecture, modern cities, hill stations, forests and beaches. Its tourism experiences span North Karnataka, the hill stations, Coastal Karnataka and South Karnataka.

Places such as Hampi, Mysore, Coorg, Udupi and Murudeshwara offer a mix of heritage, spirituality, nature and local culture.
